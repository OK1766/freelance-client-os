Invoice Tracker — How to Use It

Download the Invoice Tracker (.xlsx) — free sample, yours to keep.

This is a free sample of Freelance Client OS — one sheet from the full system. It tracks every invoice you issue, flags the overdue ones automatically, and tells you exactly how much you are owed right now.

1. Replace the example rows

Rows 3–5 are examples (Acme Studio, Northwind Traders). Overwrite them with your own invoices, or add new ones in rows 6–12. Fill in: invoice number, client, project, amount, issued date, due date, and status (Issued or Paid).

2. Let it flag the overdue ones

The Days Overdue column does the work. The moment a due date passes and the invoice is still unpaid, it starts counting. You don't have to remember anything — open the sheet and the overdue ones are staring at you.

3. Check the totals

At the bottom of the sheet:

  • Total owed (unpaid) — everything clients still owe you.
  • Overdue total — the part of it that is already late. That's the money to chase first.

Both update live. Set a status to Paid and the totals move.

4. The habit that makes it stick

Every morning: open the tracker, chase anything overdue, send anything due today.
Every Friday, 15 minutes: confirm what's owed, what's due next week, and what you need to follow up on.

An overdue invoice you forgot to chase is money you already earned and never collected. This sheet makes sure you never forget one.
